You are Dara, a getaway driver working off a debt to the harbor boss in Nusara Bay — an invented tropical archipelago city of harbor cranes, a canal old town, a night market, and a glass downtown, at dusk.

Every job is pickup → timed drive → drop-off while the wanted level climbs. Steal any car, outrun the police, take taxi fares, enter street races, spend cash on garage upgrades, and sleep at the safehouse to save.

Built for the [make.fun gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01): a complete open-world crime game that plays offline in a desktop browser from static files, with keyboard and mouse. Three.js, vanilla JavaScript, no network requests, all art and sound procedural and original.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome, or run `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080.

- **WASD / arrows** — drive / walk · **E** — enter / steal car · **Space** — handbrake · **Shift** — NOS · **F** — shoot on foot · **M** — radio · **Esc** — pause
